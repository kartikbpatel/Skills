# Lead Magnet 🧲

Complete system for coaches and consultants to create lead magnets, nurture prospects, collect payments, and close deals using structured scripts and sequences.

## When to Use

Use this skill when the user asks about:
- Creating lead magnets for coaching/consulting businesses
- Follow-up and nurturing message sequences
- Payment collection from prospects who already said yes
- Closing call scripts and frameworks
- LinkedIn outreach follow-ups
- Handling objections during sales calls
- Converting interested leads into paying clients

## Core Philosophy

Based on **$100M Offers** and **$100M Leads** by Alex Hormozi:

1. **Don't chase money** — increase certainty, reduce friction
2. **Match commitment to where the prospect is** — don't ask for more than they're ready to give
3. **Give value before asking** — lead with resources, not requests
4. **People don't buy coaching — they buy momentum** — deliver an immediate win after payment
5. **Silence sells** — after asking for payment, stop talking

## System 1: Payment Collection (Prospect Already Said Yes)

This is NOT a lead-generation problem — it's a **commitment and payment collection** problem.

### Step 1: Send a Value Message First

Don't ask for money first. Remind them why they decided to join.

```
Hi {{Name}},

Really looking forward to working together. I've started outlining a few ideas
for your client acquisition system so we can hit the ground running once we begin.

I know you mentioned you're planning to join in September, and I'm excited to
help you build a predictable system for getting qualified appointments.

Quick question...
```

### Step 2: Ask for a Small Commitment

Frame the payment as **enabling preparation**, not collecting money.

```
To secure your spot and begin preparing everything, would you be comfortable
paying the first installment or a small deposit tomorrow? That lets me block
the time and start building your roadmap before we officially kick off.
```

### Step 3: Give a Reason

People pay faster when there's a clear, truthful reason:

- "I'm onboarding only a few clients this month."
- "I want to prepare your outreach system before we start."
- "I'd like to reserve your implementation slot."

### Step 4: Deliver Immediately After Payment

Promise and deliver an immediate win:

```
As soon as the payment comes through, I'll send:

- Your personalized 90-day growth roadmap
- AI + LinkedIn playbook
- Lead generation SOP
- First outreach templates
- Access to the onboarding resources
```

### Combined Payment Request Message

```
Hi {{Name}},

Excited to start working together! I've already begun thinking through a few
ideas for your business and would love to start preparing your personalized roadmap.

To reserve your onboarding slot and get everything ready, would you be able to
make the first payment tomorrow? Once it's done, I'll immediately send your
roadmap along with the onboarding resources so we can hit the ground running.

Looking forward to working with you!
```

### If They're Hesitant

Ask an open question instead of pushing:

```
Is there anything that's holding you back from making the payment tomorrow?
Happy to work through it together.
```

This surfaces the real objection — cash flow, uncertainty, timing, or something else — so you can address it directly.

## System 2: Payment Collection Call Script

For prospects who already agreed — this is a **closing and commitment call**, not a discovery call.

### Phase 1: Build Rapport (2-3 min)

```
Hi {{Name}}! Great to see you again. How have things been since we last spoke?

(Let them talk.)

Before we dive in, I wanted to say I'm genuinely excited about working with you.
After our last conversation, I spent some time thinking about your business and
already have a few ideas that I think can create results quite quickly.
```

### Phase 2: Revisit Their Goal

```
Just so we're aligned, tell me again...

- What are you trying to achieve over the next 90 days?
- Why is that important now?
- If nothing changes over the next six months, what happens?
```

Then mirror back:

```
So if I heard you correctly, your biggest priority is getting consistent qualified
appointments so you don't have to depend on referrals or random inquiries.
```

### Phase 3: Paint the Future

```
Imagine it's 90 days from now.

- You're getting qualified calls every week.
- You know exactly where your next clients are coming from.
- Your calendar is consistently booked.

That's exactly what we're going to build together.
```

### Phase 4: Explain Your Process

```
Here's exactly what we'll do:

Phase 1 — Position your offer properly.
Phase 2 — Optimize your LinkedIn profile.
Phase 3 — Build your outreach system.
Phase 4 — Automate follow-ups using AI.
Phase 5 — Improve your sales conversations so more appointments become paying clients.

By the end, you'll have a repeatable client acquisition system instead of
depending on luck.
```

### Phase 5: Reduce Risk

```
I don't expect you to figure this out alone.

My team and I will help you through every step. We'll review your profile,
your messaging, your outreach, your follow-ups and your sales conversations.

You'll always know what to do next.
```

### Phase 6: Transition to Payment

```
Since we've already decided we're moving forward together...

The only thing left is getting your onboarding started.

(Pause.)
```

### Phase 7: Ask Naturally

```
Were you able to arrange the payment we discussed?

(Stay quiet. Silence is your best closer.)
```

### Handling Responses

**If YES:**
```
Amazing. Once it's done I'll immediately:
- Create your onboarding
- Send your roadmap
- Give you all templates
- Schedule your implementation sessions

We can literally start building your system this week.
```

Stay on Zoom. Wait for payment to complete before ending the call.

**If "I'll pay next month":**
```
Absolutely, and I'm happy to start then. Just help me understand...

Is it purely a cash-flow timing issue?
Or is there anything else that's making you hesitate?
```

**If Cash Flow:**
```
I completely understand. Rather than waiting another month and delaying
your growth... would it make sense to secure your place today with a small
deposit and pay the remaining amount on the agreed date?
```

**If They Need Time:**
```
That's completely fair. What exactly do you need time for?
```

Keep asking until you uncover the real objection.

### Final Close

```
I really believe we can help you build something predictable. And I'd rather
get started sooner than later because every month you wait is another month
of missed opportunities.

Shall we complete the payment now so I can begin preparing everything?

(Pause.)
```

### After Payment

```
Congratulations! Welcome aboard.

I'll send you:
- Your onboarding form
- Personalized 90-day roadmap
- AI & LinkedIn playbook
- WhatsApp group invite
- Session schedule
- Initial action items

We're officially getting started.
```

## System 3: Follow-Up & Nurturing Sequences

For prospects who accepted connection but haven't replied, went cold, or showed initial interest.

### Follow-up #1: No Reply After Connection

```
Hi {{First Name}},

Hope you're doing well.

I noticed many coaches are posting consistently on LinkedIn but still
struggling to get qualified appointments.

I recently put together a short guide on how some of our clients generate
30-40 qualified appointments a month using LinkedIn (without relying on ads).

Happy to share it if you'd find it useful.
```

### Follow-up #2: Value-First

```
Hi {{First Name}},

I came across your profile again and thought this might help.

One mistake I see many coaches make is focusing on content while neglecting
their outreach and follow-up systems.

I have a simple framework that explains how to build a predictable
appointment pipeline.

Let me know if you'd like me to send it over.
```

### Follow-up #3: Soft Credibility

```
Hi {{First Name}},

Just wanted to check in.

We've recently helped coaches build systems that consistently bring in
qualified conversations every month through LinkedIn.

Even if now isn't the right time, I'm happy to share a few ideas that
could help your business.

Wishing you continued success!
```

### Nurturing Cadence

Alternate between **value** and **conversation**:

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Day 1 | Share a useful guide |
| Day 4 | Ask: "What has been your biggest challenge with getting qualified appointments from LinkedIn?" |
| Day 8 | Share a case study |
| Day 15 | Check in with a new insight or framework |

### Lead Magnet Rules

1. **Don't send random PDFs** — tie every resource directly to the problem they're trying to solve
2. **One resource = one problem** — keep it focused
3. **Build trust through relevant value first** — then invite a conversation when they're ready
4. **Never repeatedly ask for a meeting** — alternate value and conversation instead

---

## Key Closing Principles

1. **Reconnect them to the outcome they want** — don't pressure into paying
2. **Show them the path to get there** — clarity reduces hesitation
3. **Make it easy to say yes** — small deposits, immediate deliverables
4. **Ask confidently, then stay silent** — silence after asking for payment is more effective than continuing to persuade
5. **Stay on the call until payment completes** — don't end the call on a promise
6. **Surface real objections** — ask open questions, not yes/no questions
7. **Frame payments as enabling preparation** — not as collecting money
